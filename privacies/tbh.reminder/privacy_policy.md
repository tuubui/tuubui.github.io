# Privacy Policy — Personal Reminder

_Last updated: 2026-08-15_

## Summary

**Personal Reminder does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.**

## Data storage

- Timers and reminders you create are stored in a local database on your device.
- App settings (theme, alarm defaults) are stored in a local configuration file on your device.
- Custom alarm sounds you pick are copied into the app's private storage on your device.
- No account is required and none can be created.

## Data collection

The app does **not**:

- collect personal information;
- track your usage or behavior (no analytics);
- show advertising;
- transmit any data to us or to third parties;
- access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files other than the sound/CSV files you explicitly pick.

## Permissions used

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Show the alarm when a timer finishes |
| Exact alarms (Android) | Ring at the precise time you set, even if the device is idle |
| Vibration | Vibrate when the alarm rings, if you enable it |
| Full-screen notification (Android) | Show the alarm screen when the device is locked |

## Import / export

The CSV export feature writes a file only where you choose to save it. The import feature reads only the file you pick. These files never leave your device unless you move them yourself.

## Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all data it stored. You can also delete individual timers in the app at any time.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same location with a new "Last updated" date.
